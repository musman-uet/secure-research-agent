# Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Password managers are recommended, and passwords must never be shared.

# Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and all cloud applications.

# Laptops
All company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

# Reporting
Report lost devices or suspected phishing emails to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour.
